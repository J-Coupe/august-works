August Works

A small studio run by James Coupe.

August Works makes short videos and stories for its own channels, including Octave Marinier, Penny Marlow and Session Thirteen.

Monstah Uploads is our in-house publishing tool. It posts our own videos to our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It isn't offered to anyone else, and it never posts to accounts we don't own.

Monstah Uploads uses YouTube API Services to upload to our YouTube channels. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of the data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Our own policy is in the Privacy policy.
