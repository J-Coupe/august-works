Privacy Policy

August Works · Last updated November 6, 2026

This policy covers Monstah Uploads, the publishing tool August Works uses to post videos to its own social media accounts.

Who uses it

Only August Works uses Monstah Uploads, and only with accounts August Works owns or manages. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

When an account owner signs in with Google (YouTube), Instagram or TikTok, we receive an access token for that account. With it, the tool can:

  • upload and publish our videos, with their titles, descriptions and captions;
  • read basic account details, such as the channel or profile name and ID;
  • read performance numbers for our own posts, such as views.

We don't read private messages, contacts, or other people's personal information.

How we store it

Tokens are kept encrypted on a computer August Works controls. We use them only to publish and measure our own content.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share data from these accounts with anyone. Videos go only to the platform they're posted on.

Google user data

Monstah Uploads' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses the YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

An account owner can revoke access at any time: in Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and in Instagram or TikTok under Settings, in the list of connected or authorized apps. We delete a revoked account's tokens. To ask us to delete anything else, email james@29brushes.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
